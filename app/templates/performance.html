Advisory & Operational Disclaimer

NewsSentinel identifies linguistic patterns learned from its training data. It does not independently verify sources, events, quotations or factual claims. Model evaluation scores reflect pattern detection accuracy on the cleaned WELFake dataset. Model confidence indicates training distribution alignment, not factual certainty.
