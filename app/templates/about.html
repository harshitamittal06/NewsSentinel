Project Dossier & Architectural Scope

About NewsSentinel

An AI-powered textual pattern analysis and misinformation detection platform engineered to analyze digital journalism, headlines, and user-submitted claims with academic rigor, mathematical transparency, and empirical accountability.

Core Operational Disclaimer & Scope

NewsSentinel identifies linguistic patterns learned from its training data. It does not independently verify sources, events, quotations or factual claims. Model predictions and confidence metrics are statistical signals generated by our calibrated Linear SVM model trained on the WELFake benchmark corpus, not definitive arbiters of truth.

Critical Limitation: Realistic Writing Style Resemblance

Empirical Evaluation Finding

During empirical manual testing, a deliberately fabricated scientific article written in realistic journalistic language was submitted to NewsSentinel. The calibrated model classified the fabricated text as Real with 73.2% confidence.

This result illustrates a fundamental limitation inherent to text-based NLP classification models: realistic writing style can closely resemble linguistic and stylometric patterns learned from genuine-news training examples.

01
Linguistic Pattern Matching vs. Fact-Checking

NewsSentinel evaluates vocabulary distribution, n-gram co-occurrences, and stylometric indicators learned from the WELFake dataset (where genuine reporting originates predominantly from wire services such as Reuters). When fabricated claims adopt neutral reporting syntax, formal attribution conventions, and clean grammatical structure, the model detects genuine-news patterns.

02
Confidence Is Not Factual Certainty

"Model Confidence" represents the calibrated posterior probability \(P(y = 1 \mid x)\) under the model's TF-IDF feature space. It measures how strongly input tokens correlate with patterns in the training data, never the factual existence of described events, validity of cited studies, or truthfulness of quotations.

03
Necessity of Editorial & Source Verification

Automated classification is an assistive decision-support tool, not an autonomous fact-checker. Highly polished disinformation, subtle falsehoods, and realistic fabrications require independent human fact-checking, primary source review, and cross-source verification.

Staged Engineering Evolution

Phase 5 Operational

NewsSentinel has been developed using an incremental, test-driven methodology to ensure data science rigor and mathematical reproducibility:

PHASE 1 & 2
Corpus Engineering, Forensic Audit & Sanitization

Ingested and standardized the WELFake master corpus (62,586 deduplicated articles). Audited dataset contamination and identified that secondary datasets (e.g. ISOT) are fully contained within WELFake, precluding invalid multi-corpus merging.

PHASE 3
Benchmark Model Training & Calibrated SVM Champion

Trained candidate models with 50,000-feature TF-IDF vectorization. Selected Platt-calibrated Linear Support Vector Machine achieving 96.59% accuracy and 0.9655 macro F1-score across 9,388 unseen test articles.

PHASE 4
Full ML Pipeline Integration & Live Web Telemetry

Integrated real-time inference engine into production FastAPI web console with synchronous deterministic stylometrics and live probability telemetry.

PHASE 5
Explainable AI, Persistence, Uncertainty & Reliability Suite

Implemented mathematically grounded lexical attribution (\(\bar{w}_j \cdot x_j\)), thread-safe SQLite WAL persistence, Bayesian uncertainty detection (<0.65 threshold), dark/light theme engine, and a 68-test automated reliability suite.

Research Objective

Problem Statement

Digital misinformation leverages sensationalism, structural hyperbole, and selective vocabulary. NewsSentinel investigates the structural, stylometric, and semantic markers that characterize fabricated claims, clickbait narratives, and authentic reporting.

By extracting stylistic signals (capitalization ratios, punctuation abuse, lexical diversity) alongside n-gram feature attributions, the system provides transparent, interpretable triage signals for media analysts and researchers.

Academic & Ethical Transparency

Governance Policy

NewsSentinel strictly rejects fabricated AI predictions, synthetic metrics, or exaggerated certainty claims. Every score represents live computed inference on empirical models.

Model limitations are explicitly highlighted throughout the user interface: ISOT is documented as being contained within WELFake, token attributions are labeled as approximate lexical contributions rather than causal truth proofs, and model confidence is clearly distinguished from factual certainty.
